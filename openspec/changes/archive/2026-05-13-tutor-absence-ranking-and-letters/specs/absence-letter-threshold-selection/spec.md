## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Absence Threshold Selection Popup
The front-end SHALL display a popup or selection menu when a tutor clicks the "Generar Carta" button for a student.

#### Scenario: Selecting absence threshold
- **WHEN** the "Generar Carta" button is clicked
- **THEN** a selection menu appears with options for 30, 60, and 90 absences.
- **AND** upon selection, the letter generation process is triggered with the selected threshold.
